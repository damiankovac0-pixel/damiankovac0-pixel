## about


I'm **18**, doing the **IB Diploma Programme May 2027**, and I like building things that actually get used.


Most of my work is around **web development, AI tools, automation, servers, and practical IT**. I build a lot together with [@VSHT3](https://github.com/VSHT3), and I also have **MoruMori** with my friend — not really public yet, but it is where we put more serious project ideas and client-style work.


I’m taking **Computer Science HL**, and my EE is currently aimed at **computer science / quantum computing**.
