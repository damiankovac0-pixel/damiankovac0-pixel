## about




I'm **18**, doing the **IB Diploma Programme May 2027**, and I like building things that actually get used.




Most of my work is around **web development, AI tools, automation, servers, and practical IT**. I build a lot together with [@VSHT3](https://github.com/VSHT3), and we also have **[MoruMori](https://github.com/morumorii)** for more serious/client-style project work.




I’m taking **Computer Science HL**, and my EE is currently aimed at **computer science / quantum computing**.
